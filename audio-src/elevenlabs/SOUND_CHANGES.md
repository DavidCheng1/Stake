# Ghost Duels: sound changes (2026-10-29)

An overview of every sound that was changed or added. The details are in [`README.md`](README.md) (plan, judging, replacement log) and in [`applied.json`](applied.json), which holds the prompt, settings and score of each new sound.

## Changed: game sound effects (`ghost-duels-web/assets/sfx/`)

17 of the 22 sound effects were replaced with sounds generated by ElevenLabs. Each one was trimmed and matched to the old file's loudness. The old version of each is kept as `audio-src/sfx-old/<name>_old.mp3`, outside the game folder, so it is never uploaded or played.

| Sound | When it plays | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `click` | Any button press | Soft synthesized click | Crisp wooden tap with a bright tick |
| `spin` | Spin pressed (every round) | Ghostly gust, low whoomp and a faint "ooh" | Instant hard hit with a bone rattle; shorter, so it never drags |
| `land` | A reel stops (7 per spin) | Low coffin-lid knock | Sharp single knock with more mid-range, so it carries on phone speakers |
| `land_heavy` | A VS reel or crate slams down | Stone slab with rattling chains | Tight heavy boom that hits at once |
| `charge` | The small VS charges up | Eerie theremin rise over a hiss | Rising energy sweep that grows louder and cuts off at its peak, into the burst |
| `burst` | The VS reel bursts open; also the duel slam | Thunder, smoke blast and a ringing shard | Full-bodied boom with a lightning crack, instant |
| `clash` | Duel fighters collide | Crossing blades and metallic ringing | Heavier metal impact with ringing |
| `wail` | A fighter is knocked out | Descending ghost "oooh" | Punchy hit, then a long falling ghost wail |
| `challenger` | The ghost pirate enters a duel | Low ship's horn over a dark organ | Cannon boom, then horn, chains and organ |
| `sting` | The pirate wins a duel | Choir "aah" chord with a bell | Instant bell hit and arpeggio into a chord |
| `sting_devil` | The devil wins a duel or enters; on top of the max win | Tritone choir, organ pedal and a growl | Massive demonic hit that lands at once and rings long |
| `banner` | A banner appears | Rising whoosh into a bell | Fast whoosh into a bright bell, more snap |
| `bigwin` | Big win (20× bet or more) | Toll, choir, organ and a cascade of bells | **Money payout:** cash register "cha-ching" and a cascade of coins into the tray, with a ringing bell |
| `fanfare` | Free spins triggered | Organ swell, church toll, choir and falling bells | Big explosion straight into a bright bell arpeggio (a silent gap between them was cut out) |
| `fs_land` | A scatter (FS) symbol lands | Magical scatter sample, sharpened by hand | **Reverted (2026-10-30):** the magical scatter sample is back, by request. The ElevenLabs take `fs_land_t3.mp3` stays in `takes/` |
| `edge` | Tease when one FS is missing | Designed drone with a heartbeat | Builds steadily and peaks at 2.4 s, then stops, as the tease needs |
| `crumble` | Bonus end: runes crumble | Grinding stone and falling grit | Short, dry stone crumble |

`bigwin` went through two versions on the same day: an orchestral jackpot stinger first, then the money payout. Only the original and the money payout are kept.

## Added

| What | Where | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio intro glint sound | `ghost-duels-web/loader.js` | Airy shimmer that follows the glint across the Maestro Studios logo, a soft bell "ting" and a tiny ping per star. Synthesized in code, no file. It follows the saved mute and effects volume. Browsers only allow it without a click where the page may autoplay sound; otherwise it plays after a tap during the logo. |

## Changed: music behaviour

| What | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background music ("Spooky Carousel") | Started with the click on the intro screen | Silent during the studio logo, then fades in as the game appears. Where the browser still blocks sound until a click, it starts with that click, as before. The music file itself is unchanged. |

## Not changed

| Sound | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `chime`, `crystal` | The game builds win melodies from them, so they need an exact single A5 |
| `toll` | The game needs exactly A2 (it also plays it an octave up); ElevenLabs ignored the note |
| `tumble` | 11 takes in 3 rounds: all thin high hiss or a tiny blip; it plays on every win |
| `heartbeat` | 11 takes in 3 rounds: mostly deep bass, weaker on phones than the current one |

## Rolling a sound back

```powershell
copy audio-src\sfx-old\spin_old.mp3 ghost-duels-web\assets\sfx\spin.mp3
```
