Background for an original 1980s neon night slot game, wide landscape, painted to be animated later: the centre of the
picture is calm and simple (the reel grid will sit over it): a big striped synthwave sun setting into a calm ocean, its
reflection shimmering on the water. Left and right, framing the scene: tall palm trees with big fronds, art-deco hotel
towers with glowing neon outlines and abstract neon shapes (no readable signs), a few lit windows. A deep violet night
sky with stars and soft clouds, a seaside boulevard along the bottom edge with pink and cyan light trails of passing
cars. Elements clearly separated so they can move later: palms that can sway, waves that can roll, neon that can
flicker, trails that can stream. Polished stylized painting in the same 1980s cartoon cel look (flat two-tone shading,
clean bold outlines), hot pink, cyan, sunset orange, deep violet. No text, no letters, no logos, no cars, no people.
