The Chase

3, 4 or 5 sunsets anywhere start 10, 12 or 15 free spins. A reel holds at most one sunset. Every car that lands stays and drives one reel to the left before each spin. A car that drives off the board gives +1 spin (Nitro). Sunsets don't land during The Chase, so it can't start again from inside.

Ways

Wins pay for 3, 4 or 5 matching symbols on neighbouring reels from the left, in any row (3125 ways). Values below are per way at your bet; ways add up, cars multiply. Every win pays at least 0.10× the bet.

Bonus

The Bonus button opens: The Chase for × the bet (10 free spins), Super Chase for × the bet (10 free spins, every car ×3 or more), and Chance ×3: while it is on, The Chase starts about 3× as often and every spin costs × the bet. Each one shows its cost and asks you to confirm.

Return and maximum win

Expected return about 96 % in every mode: base game, Chance ×3, The Chase and Super Chase. Maximum win 10,000× the bet in every mode; a round ends when it is reached.

Controls

Spin (the round arrow, or the space bar) plays one round. ‹ › lower and raise the bet. Autoplay (circular arrow): choose a number of spins and press Start; press it again or the space bar to stop. Turbo (lightning): faster animations, same results. Bonus: the bonus menu. Menu (three lines): game info, music and effects volume, and sound on or off for every sound. A tap during a win or a message skips it.
