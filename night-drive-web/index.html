The Chase

3, 4 or 5 sunsets anywhere start 10, 12 or 15 free spins. A reel holds at most one sunset. Every car that lands stays and drives one reel to the left before each spin. A car that drives off the board gives +1 spin (Nitro). Sunsets don't land during The Chase, so there are no re-triggers.

Ways

Wins pay for 3, 4 or 5 matching symbols on neighbouring reels from the left, in any row (3125 ways). Values below are per way at your bet; ways add up, cars multiply. Every win pays at least 0.10× the bet, and each win is rounded to the nearest 0.10× the bet.

Controls

Spin (the big white button with two arrows, or the space bar) plays one round. The arrows ˅ ˄ next to the bet lower and raise the bet; the line under it shows where the bet sits between the lowest and the highest. Autoplay (the round button with a circular arrow and a play sign): choose a number of spins and press Start; press it again or the space bar to stop. Turbo (the round button with a lightning bolt): faster animations, same results. While Chance ×3 is on, its tag on the left shows the price per spin and Turn off switches it off. Bonus (the square with the studio logo, left of the bar): the bonus menu. Menu (three lines): game info, music and effects volume, and sound on or off for every sound. A tap during a win or a message skips it.

Malfunction voids all wins and plays. A consistent internet connection is required. In the event of a disconnection, reload the game to finish any uncompleted rounds.
